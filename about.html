About Us

Empowering learners worldwide with accessible, high-quality education from YouTube

Our Story

Founded in 2020, EduLearn began with a simple mission: make quality education accessible to everyone, everywhere. We curate the best YouTube educational content into structured learning paths, serving over 100,000 students across the globe.

We believe that education should be practical, engaging, and led by industry professionals who bring real-world experience into every lesson.

Our Mission

To democratize education by curating the best free YouTube educational content into structured, expert-led courses that equip learners with the skills they need to succeed in the modern workforce.
